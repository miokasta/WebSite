About

MIOKASTA is a Toronto-based DJ and electronic music producer blending melodic house, progressive house, tech house and melodic techno. His sound combines hypnotic grooves, emotional melodies and driving dancefloor energy. He is the founder of Euphoria Sonics and Rapture Tech House—independent projects connecting original music, events and broadcast DJ sets. His releases have appeared on Progressive Vibes Music, dublucid, GAME OF TONES and Flowing Movement Music.
